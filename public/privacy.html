Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024 · Applies to: AdView web application

1. What we collect

AdView collects the minimum information needed to operate the reward system:

  • Email address — used as your account identifier
  • Password — stored as a bcrypt hash (we never store plain-text passwords)
  • Point balance — your current earned and redeemed points
  • Reward events — timestamps and point amounts for each ad reward
  • Redemption requests — items requested and their status
  • Session tokens — hashed tokens stored in a local SQLite database

2. How we use your data

We use your data solely to operate the AdView service: authenticating you, crediting and tracking your points, and processing redemption requests. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising profiling.

3. Google Ad Manager

When Google Ad Manager is enabled, Google's ad and consent technologies may process data under Google's Privacy Policy. You should add a consent-management platform (CMP) before serving production rewarded ads to real users.

4. Cookies & sessions

AdView sets a single rl_session HTTP-only cookie to maintain your login session. The token is valid for 7 days. No third-party tracking cookies are set by AdView itself.

5. Data retention

Your data is retained until you delete your account. We do not have an automated deletion pipeline in this starter — add one before a public launch.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
