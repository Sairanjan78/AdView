The Two Big Hollywood Releases on December 18

Avengers: Doomsday

Marvel Studios has scheduled Avengers: Doomsday for December 18, 2026 in the United States. Indian audiences are scheduled to see it one day earlier, on December 17, in English, Hindi, Tamil and Telugu, according to the India release announcement reported in October. The film is directed by Anthony and Joe Russo and features Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans and Chris Hemsworth among its large ensemble cast.

The story brings heroes from different corners of the Marvel universe into a confrontation with a major threat. If you want to catch up before the film, revisiting the earlier Avengers films and the relevant Fantastic Four and X-Men stories may add context, but check Marvel's official film information for the latest details.

Official information: Marvel Studios - Avengers: Doomsday.

Dune: Part Three

Dune: Part Three, directed by Denis Villeneuve, is scheduled for December 18, 2026 in the United States. Current Indian release listings also show December 18. In India, paid previews in IMAX and other premium formats are announced from December 16; these previews are not the same as the listed general release date of December 18. The film continues the screen adaptation of Frank Herbert's Dune saga, with Timothée Chalamet and Zendaya returning among the principal cast.

The December 18 pairing of Doomsday and Dune has been nicknamed “Dunesday” by fans and entertainment coverage. Treat it as a playful label for the scheduling coincidence, not an official event or a studio-created release campaign.

For release updates, check Warner Bros. and your local cinema listings.

Indian Cinema: King Arrives on December 24

Shah Rukh Khan's King, directed by Siddharth Anand, is scheduled to release in Indian cinemas on December 24, 2026. The cast includes Shah Rukh Khan, Suhana Khan, Deepika Padukone and Abhishek Bachchan. The makers' countdown post and current Indian listings identify December 24 as the release date.

Do not confuse a domestic release listing with a confirmed date for every international territory. Overseas dates and local language or certification details should be checked with the distributor and the cinema in that country.

What About Ticket Prices?

There is no single December 2026 ticket price. In India, the amount depends on the city, cinema chain, auditorium, format, seat category, showtime and booking fees. A premium-format opening-night ticket can cost much more than a standard 2D weekday show, so one unusually high price should never be presented as the normal price for all viewers.

For an accurate quote, search the exact film and date in your local cinema or ticket-booking app. Compare the final checkout amount, not just the displayed base fare, and check whether convenience fees or taxes are added. Prices and showtimes are live inventory and may change.

How to Plan Your December Movie Calendar

  • Check the country first: US, India and other territories can have different opening days.
  • Confirm the format: IMAX, 4DX, Dolby and standard screenings can have different prices and availability.
  • Check language options: Indian cinemas may list English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and other dubbed versions separately.
  • Use the cinema's current listing: Release calendars are useful for planning, but the local cinema page is the best place to confirm actual showtimes and seats.
  • Recheck close to release: Distributors can move dates or adjust the number of screens.

How Do India Dates Differ from US Dates?

Indian and US releases do not always line up. Avengers: Doomsday opens in India on December 17, a day ahead of its December 18 US date, while King is dated December 24 in the UK and India; a US theatrical date was not confirmed in the sources checked. The Angry Birds Movie 3 is listed for December 23 in the US, December 25 in the UK and January 1, 2027 in India, so it belongs in a January plan for Indian audiences.

These differences usually reflect local holidays, certification schedules and distributor decisions. A date that is firm in one country can still move in another, which is why this guide labels each entry with the market it applies to. Readers who travel between markets should check the cinema listing for the exact city on the day they plan to watch, rather than relying on a single global release calendar.
